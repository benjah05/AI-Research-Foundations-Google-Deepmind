<h1>Gogle Deepmind: AI Research Foundations</h1>
<p>AI Research Foundations is an eight-course curriculum which exposes learners to foundational building blocks of modern AI through the lens of language modeling. Research skills and responsible innovation are central to each of the courses, so that learners are empowered to create high impact and responsible AI solutions globally. The curriculum builds up to a capstone project where learners research and develop AI-backed solutions to challenges that they recognize.</p>
<h2>The AI Research Foundations Courses</h2>
<p>The eight courses in the curriculum are: </p>
<ul>
<li>Build Your Own Small Language Model</li>
<li>Represent Your Language Data</li>
<li>Design and Train Neural Networks</li>
<li>Discover the Transformer Architecture</li>
<li>Finetune Your Model</li>
<li>Align Your Model</li>
<li>Accelerate Your Model</li>
<li>Capstone: Develop Your Model for Real-World Impact</li>
</ul>
<br><br>
<p>Google Deepmind's AI Research Foundations curriculum will deepen your understanding of the AI technologies behind language models like Google Gemini, and will teach you how to build and fine-tune modern language models from the ground up. It is designed for university students and community learners who are proficient in python, and studying computer science, math, physics or other technical subjects.</p>
